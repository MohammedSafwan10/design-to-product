# Independent showcase review v2

24 October 2026. Independent **model critique**, not a human jury, user study or
frozen paired benchmark. Builder rationale/provenance was not read before the
visual judgment. Product names and file paths were visible: this was unblinded.

Captured all 12 new showcases in isolated Chromium at 390×844 and 1440×900,
reduced motion enabled, and viewed the entry screenshots/contact sheets. Also
captured/viewed six earlier representative entries at 1440×900. Exercised seven
bounded local actions and viewed the music/tactical result frames. New examples
and older examples have different briefs/budgets, so this is not causal evidence
that a skill revision wins. Browser studies establish no native/gameplay behavior.

Structured observations and exact screenshot filenames are in
[`showcase-review-v2.json`](../skills/design-to-product/evals/showcase-review-v2.json).
Local artifacts are under `skills/design-to-product/evals/artifacts/independent-v2/` and included as original review evidence.

## Material findings

1. **Letterpress phone clipping:** the word “impression.” is visibly cut at the
   right edge, with a horizontal scrollbar; root overflow measured 43px. Reflow
   or reduce the phone display size and contain tilted artwork safely.
2. **Science phone labels:** chart axes shrink to tiny unreadable glyphs within
   a tall mostly blank chart area. Recompose the mobile plot with readable labels
   and height; add a table if needed. Shrinking an SVG is not chart adaptation.
3. **Dispatch phone task distance:** the large masthead/proposition occupies
   roughly 350px and selected decision detail follows two exception cards. Compact
   the phone header and bring detail/action close to the selected exception.
4. **Inventory phone task distance:** branding and all stock cards precede
   checkout. Compact the branding and consider a persistent selected-part summary.
5. **Player/transit lower actions:** player controls meet bottom navigation near
   the phone fold; transit's start action follows route steps below the fold.
   Check short heights, large text, safe areas and focus before deciding whether
   a persistent action improves the task. These observations are not failures by
   themselves; intentional scrolling can be appropriate.

## What improved visually

The botanical plant/serif treatment, fluorescent material yard, kinetic bicycle
poster and letterpress artifact differ in hierarchy, imagery and composition,
not merely palette. Audio timeline, spectral plot, route planner, stock checkout
and tactical briefing also represent distinct tasks. Compared with the older
shared pale-field/panel specimens, the new collection has visibly broader craft
vocabulary. Do not turn that subjective observation into “proven better.”

## Bounded behavior and honesty

Seven local actions produced visible changes and no runtime issues in captured
sessions. Validation appeared for inventory selection and night-ride lights.
Music explicitly reported unavailable audio; booking preserved selection and
said no reservation occurred; tactical review showed fuel arithmetic while
stating no mission was launched. These are appropriate study boundaries.
The source register's external research count must not include these original
local screens, and this review must not be described as a human comparison.

No full-flow keyboard, screen-reader, native-device, controller, gameplay or
participant task testing was performed by this reviewer. Remaining render/task
limits keep the entries candidates until the relevant review gates are met.
