## What changes the craft guidance

- Differentiate typography by editorial reading, expressive display, product
  controls, data comparison and game viewing conditions. A renamed font with the
  same size ramp is not a differentiated system.
- Use real glyph/script/fallback specimens, optical roles, numerals, long labels
  and text enlargement before selecting a family. Font axis counts are not taste.
- Derive composition from a subject or task. Public Theater's account connects
  street type to its mission; Duolingo's historical account connects structure
  to the next lesson. Borrow the reasoning process, not either visual identity.
- Specify image subject, viewpoint, focal point and crop across widths. IBM's
  consistency discipline transfers; its brand-specific photography rules do not
  become universal bans.
- Separate categorical, ordered and status colors. Carbon's examples supply a
  semantic distinction, not an automatic certification of our palettes.
- Keep platform readability and navigation alongside originality. Government,
  data and game contexts need different evidence and different compositions.

## Independent local showcase review protocol

Inspect screenshots before builder rationale; use anonymous screen codes where
possible. Record viewport, screenshot artifact, immediate hierarchy impression,
specific visible faults and a bounded local-flow observation. Then reveal the
surface/task to assess appropriateness. Different products are craft reviews,
not controlled A/B evidence. Browser-native appearance is not native/device or
gameplay verification. Record unresolved defects instead of inventing scores.

Local showcase observations are appended after captures exist. The fourteen
source records above must never be described as completed user/design comparisons.
