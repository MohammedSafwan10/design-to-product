reading. This is a seed research pass, not a gallery study or visual benchmark.

## Actual coverage

Eleven substantive sources were read: five foundations and six first-party
product descriptions, one for each planned surface. Two additional foundation
pages returned JavaScript shells and remain unverified. No screenshot critique,
live product task, authenticated session, native-device test, model comparison
or human study was performed. The proposed 72 product cases are **not collected**.

## Rights and next collection

Only links and original concise notes are bundled. No source media, fonts,
screenshots, logos or code was imported. Do not infer redistribution rights from
public access, or adopt another skill's proprietary dataset. Check actual terms
before capture, bulk collection or publication. Product descriptions can seed
hypotheses; they cannot license copying distinctive artwork or establish quality.

Next: expand the convenience sample by task/density/input/language, include
ordinary effective products and counterexamples, then record original render
observations and authorized tasks. Each record needs source/version/date,
viewport/input, states, method, principle, counterexample, uncertainty and rights.
Keep standards, first-party descriptions, measured renders, model judgments and
human evidence distinct. Review research-derived constraints against real tasks.
