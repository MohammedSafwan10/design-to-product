# Imagery and art direction

Art direction is a coherent choice of subject, viewpoint, material, framing,
lighting, type and behavior. It is not an image quota or an effects preset.

## Capability-aware asset choices

Inspect the host's actual tools and existing files first. Do not infer image
generation, browsing or visual inspection from a model name. A text-focused
model can orchestrate an image tool; a model without that tool must not pretend
it generated or visually inspected an asset. Use available applicable image
skills/tool instructions when generating or editing raster artwork.

| Available material/capability | Next action |
| --- | --- |
| Suitable project or user-owned assets | Inspect and reuse; preserve identity, provenance and intended subject. |
| Authorized image generation, such as GPT Image via a host tool | Generate missing artwork when it materially improves the brief. Write an image brief, inspect results and integrate responsive exports. Do not generate needless decoration or assume paid service access. |
| Browsing/download access, no generation | Seek suitable licensed assets when appropriate; record source, permissions and credits. Do not substitute generic stock for required real product/customer evidence. |
| Code output only | Use deliberate typography, real content, layout, or original SVG/CSS/canvas diagrams when those meet the brief. Simple icons and diagrams are valid; random blobs or emoji are not a substitute for required rich artwork. |
| Essential specific artwork is unavailable | Ask for exactly what is missing: for example the actual product photograph, logo master or an approved image source. Explain its role and needed crop/format; continue layout, interaction and other independent work. |

Avoid asking the user to search for images the agent can appropriately source or
generate itself. Conversely, do not invent brand photography, customer evidence
or likenesses simply to avoid a necessary asset request. If a text-only design
genuinely meets the task, it can be a complete direction; missing artwork is not
an automatic reason to add images or block progress.

Placeholders are a scoped prototyping tool, not a finished visual strategy. Use
them only when the requested stage permits, label their role in the preview and
asset manifest, and keep the replacement task explicit. Do not present a gray
box, dummy photograph or imitation product render as completed required artwork.
If generation fails, report that and use the next appropriate option; do not
silently downgrade an approved art-led direction into generic text/cards.

## Make a subject-specific image brief

Inventory owned material, product output, context, useful diagrams and licensed
sources. State what each image proves: construction, scale, atmosphere, workflow,
comparison or identity. Use the strongest available evidence. Generated artwork
can support a concept but cannot establish real customers, manufactured products
or completed events. A task screen can need no artwork at all.

For each asset specify subject, viewpoint, focal point, light, material, crop,
text-safe area, responsive behavior, alpha and delivery budget. Keep interactive
labels and domain state native. Generate isolated components when layering helps
responsive composition; inspect edges and alpha on actual backgrounds.

[IBM photography guidance](https://www.ibm.com/design/language/photography/tips-and-techniques/)
demonstrates deliberate perspective, focal points and common crop proportions.
Its eye-level/aerial and lighting preferences belong to IBM's identity, not a
universal ban on other photography. Transfer the discipline of a consistent
viewpoint and visible subject, not its brand-specific prescription.

## Crop with meaning

Name the focal subject and preserve the detail that carries the claim. Plan
phone and wide crops rather than letting `cover` choose accidentally. Avoid
cutting joints in a construction explanation, controls in a product interface,
faces without intention or text needed to understand the image. Use `contain`
when the whole artifact matters. Review final compressed files, not only masters.

A large picture with no useful subject is not stronger than a small informative
one. A gallery can communicate variety when examples materially differ; repeated
placeholders and recolored duplicates cannot prove variety. Code-native diagrams
are valuable when they explain actual structure and clearly label sample data.

## Color follows meaning

Define canvas/ink/action/surface/focus/status pairs; measure actual pair contrast
in each state. Choose a palette related to the subject and audience while
preserving learned semantics and non-color cues. Do not tint every status with
the brand accent. [Carbon's chart-color guidance](https://www.carbondesignsystem.com/building-blocks/data-visualization/color-palettes)
distinguishes categorical, sequential and diverging roles. A categorical chart
needs distinguishable categories; a sequential scale needs ordered magnitude.
Its brand tokens are examples, not automatic accessible replacements for your
own measured chart/background/label combinations.

## Brand continuity without sameness

Carry a few distinctive decisions through related screens: type voice, crop,
material, alignment or a meaningful artifact. Adapt composition to the task.
[Vitsœ's presentation of Rams's principles](https://www.vitsoe.com/gb/about/good-design)
connects usefulness, comprehension and aesthetic care; it is a practitioner
position illustrated by products, not an experiment proving a preferred style.
[Duolingo's path account](https://blog.duolingo.com/new-duolingo-home-screen-design/)
ties organization to a next lesson; do not transplant its path/characters into
unrelated products or claim its learning results as evidence for our library.

## Provenance and review

Record author/source/license, release/version, allowed transformations and
credits before shipping. Public access grants no blanket rights to logos,
screenshots, typefaces or photographs. Keep generated provenance explicit and
source masters separate from production exports. No external imagery or fonts
were bundled for the research behind this reference.

Inspect a contact sheet for cohesion and full-size assets for defects. Review
phone/wide crops, loading/failure states, focus and motion alternatives. Record
specific visible faults. Do not give an automatic AI-slop percentage or infer
poor design from a hue, gradient, card or typeface alone.
